**Design Movement:** Dark Brutalism meets Digital Archaeology
**Core Principles:**
- Raw, exposed structure — code as artifact, not decoration
- High contrast monochrome with acid-yellow accent (like caution tape in a vault)
- Asymmetric, off-grid layouts that feel like excavation site maps
- Heavy typographic hierarchy — oversized labels, tiny metadata

**Signature Elements:**
- Thick horizontal rules as "exhibit dividers"
- Monospaced exhibit numbers in oversized type (EXHIBIT_001)
- Glitch/scanline overlay on hero

**Interaction Philosophy:** Hover reveals hidden metadata panels that slide in from the side. Clicking "enters" the exhibit with a full-screen takeover.

**Animation:** Glitch transitions, scanline wipe reveals, typewriter text.

**Typography System:** Space Mono (monospace) for labels + Bebas Neue for display headings. No serif.

**Design Movement:** Neo-Noir Futurism / Digital Noir Museum
**Core Principles:**
- Deep space darkness with cool-blue bioluminescent glow
- Glass morphism panels as "display cases" with frosted borders
- Spotlight radial gradients on each exhibit card
- Cinematic proportions — wide letterbox hero, tall exhibit panels

**Signature Elements:**
- Radial spotlight glow behind each project card
- Glass-morphism card borders with subtle inner glow
- Exhibit number plaques (Roman numerals or padded zeros)

**Interaction Philosophy:** Cards lift with perspective tilt on hover. Scroll-triggered entrance animations (fade-up + blur-out). Smooth section transitions.

**Animation:** Framer Motion scroll-linked opacity/transform. Staggered card reveals. Cursor spotlight that follows mouse.

**Typography System:** Cormorant Garamond (display, elegant serif) for headings + DM Mono for code/labels + Inter for body. Creates a classical-meets-digital tension.

## Selected Design: Neo-Noir Futurism / Digital Noir Museum

The second approach best captures the "digital museum" concept with:
- Dark atmosphere evoking a museum at night
- Glass display cases for project exhibits
- Spotlight effects on cards
- Elegant typography pairing (Cormorant Garamond + DM Mono)
- Cinematic scroll animations
